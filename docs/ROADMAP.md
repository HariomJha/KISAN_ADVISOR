# Roadmap

## Design principles (most users are new to technology)
- Local language first; big buttons; icon plus words; dropdowns and map taps instead of typing.
- Few steps per screen; remember the last answers; always show a clear "Help" route (WhatsApp / call-back).
- Low data use: light pages, no heavy images; works on cheap Android phones.
- Honest numbers: show data source and date; keep the disclaimer visible; mark sample data.
- Assisted mode: a village volunteer ("Krishi Mitra") can use the app on a farmer's behalf.

## Phase 1: multi-page site (this release)
Home, Services hub, Best-crop (state, district, village, map pin), About, Contact; 12 languages (Hindi and English complete; the other 10 are partial drafts that fall back to English); districts API with sample data.

## Phase 1.5: quick wins
Read-aloud button for results; WhatsApp "Help" button; remember last inputs; full official district list; native-speaker review of every language; Punjabi and others as needed.

## Phase 2: accounts and information
Sign up / login (email or Google first, phone OTP later); saved fields; blog and news (agriculture research, in simple language); contact form saved to database with admin view; weather forecast and alerts for the farmer's pin; mandi prices page (Agmarknet); government schemes guide with links to official sites (PM-KISAN, crop insurance, Kisan Credit Card, Soil Health Card, e-NAM); crop calendar by district; Alembic migrations.

## Phase 3: help from people and from photos
Plant disease check (photo upload, likely diseases, confidence, expert review); ask-an-expert questions and call-back requests; expert dashboard; short local-language videos; soil-testing lab locator; saved advice history.

## Phase 4: marketplace (check licensing rules first)
Dealer directory and enquiry button; verified seller sign-up (seed and pesticide licences); listings for seeds, pesticides, equipment; equipment rental and custom-hiring directory; orders and payments only after legal review; ratings and complaint handling.

## Phase 5: reach and intelligence
Voice assistant in local languages; SMS / missed-call advisory; offline-capable PWA; farmer community and group buying through FPOs; market and cold-storage linkage; loan and insurance guidance; learning from real farmer outcomes to improve recommendations.

## Safety and compliance checklist
- Pesticide advice only from products registered in India, with label dose and safety notes, reviewed by an expert.
- Seed and pesticide sale needs the right dealer licences; check the rules before taking payments.
- Personal data (phone numbers, locations, photos): collect the minimum, state the purpose, protect access; check the requirements of India's data protection law.
- Content from universities and government sites: follow their reuse terms and credit sources.
